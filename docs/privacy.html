Privacy policy

Arquemestre — measuring and design for woodshops

Effective October 25, 2026 · updated October 31, 2026 (sending a cutting plan)

Summary: Arquemestre has no sign-up, doesn't send your data to any server of ours and shows no ads. Everything you create stays on your own device.

1. What data the app uses

  • Notes, sketches, photos, videos, measurements, furniture projects, quotes and schedule that you create. They are kept on your device and are not sent to any server of ours. If you use the system's automatic backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google on Android), the system itself may include these files in your backup, according to your settings.
  • Photo library: on iPhone, photos you take with the in-app camera are also saved to your photo library (with your permission), so they are never lost.
  • Camera and photos, only when you ask, to place a photo inside a note or a project.
  • Microphone, only while you record a video of the room to keep with the client's project.
  • Bluetooth, to connect a laser measure (for example Bosch) and receive the measurement, and to show a project to another device nearby (on iPhone, through the local peer-to-peer network). The app does not use Bluetooth or the local network to find your location.
  • Notifications, to remind you of the appointments you scheduled.

2. What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, e-mail, phone number or location.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

3. Subscription and free trial

Access to the app is through a monthly auto-renewing subscription (the price shown by the store in your country), with a free trial for people who have never subscribed. On iPhone and iPad the subscription goes through Apple's App Store; on Android, through Google Play. Payment, renewal and cancellation are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. The app only receives confirmation that the subscription is active; it never receives card or payment details.

To cancel: on iPhone, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

4. Backup and sharing

The app can save a copy of your data on the device itself (iPhone: the Arquemestre folder in the Files app; Android: Downloads/ArqueMeasure), and you can create a backup file (ZIP). When you share a PDF, image or message (for example via WhatsApp), it goes to the app you choose, and from then on that app's policy applies.

Sending a cutting plan: when you tap “Send project” in the cutting plan, the project (dimensions, parts and finishes, without prices) is compressed inside the link itself that you send via WhatsApp or another app. The link opens a page of ours (hosted on GitHub Pages) that only hands the project to the recipient's app; the part of the link that holds the project stays on the device and is not sent to any server (as with any website, GitHub may log the page visit, such as the IP address). For someone who just installed the app, the page copies the link: on iPhone, on first launch the app shows a Paste button and only reads the copied content if the person taps it; on Android, during the first launches the app checks whether the copied text is a cutting-plan link and, if so, opens the project and clears the clipboard. None of this leaves the device.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete notes and projects inside the app, or delete everything by uninstalling the app. On iPhone, the automatic copy lives in the app's folder in Files and is removed when you uninstall. On Android, the copy in Downloads/ArqueMeasure stays until you delete that folder.

6. Children

The app is a work tool for professionals and is not directed at children.

7. Contact

The app requires no login or account.
