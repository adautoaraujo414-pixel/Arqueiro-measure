Arquemestre help

Arquemestre is the measuring, design, cutting, quote and scheduling app for woodshops and cabinet makers. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Contact us: adautoaraujo414@gmail.com
Tell us your device model and, if you can, send a screenshot.

How do I connect the Bosch laser measure?

Turn the laser on and enable its Bluetooth (check the laser's manual for the button). In the app, open a note and tap Bosch laser at the top; the laser shows up in the list. Once connected, tap a dimension and measure: the reading drops in by itself.

I don't have a Bluetooth laser. Does it work?

Yes. Tap the measurement of any dimension and type it on the app's keypad. You can even add and subtract (e.g. 2750−4).

How do I change the language?

In the library, tap ⋯ and 🌐 Language. The app comes in English, Português and Español; in automatic mode it follows the device language.

How much does it cost?

A monthly subscription (the price the store shows in your country), with a free trial for people who have never subscribed. It renews automatically every month until you cancel.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone/iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Arquemestre › Cancel Subscription (or, in the app, ⋯ › Manage subscription).
Android: Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Arquemestre › Cancel (or, in the app, ⋮ › Manage subscription).
If you cancel before the free trial ends, you won't be charged. After canceling, access continues until the end of the month already paid.

I subscribed and the app asked again

On the subscription screen, tap Already subscribed · restore, using the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you subscribed with.

How do I move my data to another device?

In the library, tap ⋯ → Full backup (ZIP) and keep the file. On the new device, tap ⋯ → Restore backup and pick the file.

How do I delete my data?

Delete notes and projects inside the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything. Nothing is stored on any server of ours. See the privacy policy.
